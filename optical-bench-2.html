Detector (CCD)

Optical elements

The first lens is the objective. Adds a collimator (parallel beam), a tube lens and, if ticked, an ocular with the eye at its exit pupil. Otherwise the CCD sits at the primary image plane.

Ray-trace layout

+ = upright, − = inverted relative to the object. Moc is the angular magnification against the unaided eye at 250 mm. After changing an f, press the relay button again to re-place the lenses.
Wheel = zoom both axes (Shift: x only, Alt: y only); drag = pan. Rays are coloured by wavelength; dashed = diffracted orders; teal dashed lines = principal planes H, H′ of thick lenses.

Object and detector image

Both views look along the direction of the light, ±30 mm. One converging lens gives an inverted, reversed image (rotated 180°); two imaging stages bring it upright.
